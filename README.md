CHICKEN PROJECT: A TRIBUTE TO STARDEW VALLEY

📽️ ANIMATION PREVIEW



🚀 THE TRIBUTE

This repository contains a technical "derivation" and style study of one of the most iconic sprites in indie games.
To expand my skills and explore new artistic horizons, I decided to recreate this classic art

🎨 The Art: A recreation of the Stardew Valley chicken, focusing on volume training.

🔧 The Tool: Created and animated exclusively in Piskel.

⚖️ The Credits: The original character design belongs to ConcernedApe and Chucklefish. This is a study made by a fan (me), non-profit, created for educational and portfolio purposes.

🎉 The Goal: To diversify MexicanPixel's portfolio, honoring creators who inspire the indie community and inspire me.
